# Fundamental Analysis Workflow

A structured approach to evaluating a company's intrinsic value through financial statement analysis.

## When to Use

- Evaluating a potential investment
- Understanding a company's financial health
- Preparing for earnings analysis
- Due diligence on a business

## Workflow Steps

### Phase 1: Company Context

**Objective:** Understand what the company does and its market position.

1. Fetch company overview
2. Identify:
   - Business model
   - Revenue sources
   - Competitive position
   - Industry dynamics

**Key Questions:**
- How does this company make money?
- What's the competitive moat?
- Is this a growth or value company?

### Phase 2: Income Statement Analysis

**Objective:** Assess profitability and earnings quality.

1. Fetch income statements (3-5 years)
2. Analyze:
   - Revenue growth trajectory
   - Gross margin stability
   - Operating leverage (margin expansion with growth)
   - Net income vs operating income
   - EPS growth vs share count changes

**Key Questions:**
- Is revenue growing? At what rate?
- Are margins expanding or contracting?
- Is earnings growth from operations or financial engineering?

**Red Flags:**
- Revenue growing but income declining
- Volatile margins
- Large gap between GAAP and non-GAAP earnings
- Declining gross margins

### Phase 3: Balance Sheet Analysis

**Objective:** Assess financial strength and capital allocation.

1. Fetch balance sheets (3-5 years)
2. Analyze:
   - Asset composition and quality
   - Liability structure (short vs long-term)
   - Equity trends
   - Working capital management

**Key Questions:**
- Is the company well-capitalized?
- Can it meet short-term obligations?
- Is debt manageable?
- How is capital being deployed?

**Red Flags:**
- Declining cash position
- Rising short-term debt
- Goodwill > 25% of assets
- Receivables growing faster than revenue
- Inventory buildup

### Phase 4: Cash Flow Analysis

**Objective:** Verify earnings quality through cash generation.

1. Fetch cash flow statements (3-5 years)
2. Analyze:
   - Operating cash flow vs net income
   - Free cash flow trends
   - Capital expenditure requirements
   - Capital return to shareholders

**Key Questions:**
- Is the company generating real cash?
- Is free cash flow positive and growing?
- How is cash being used?

**Red Flags:**
- Net income >> Operating cash flow
- Negative free cash flow
- Heavy reliance on debt financing
- Declining OCF despite growing revenue

### Phase 5: Ratio Analysis

**Objective:** Quantify financial performance and compare to benchmarks.

Calculate and trend:

**Profitability:**
- Gross margin
- Operating margin
- Net margin
- ROE, ROA, ROIC

**Liquidity:**
- Current ratio
- Quick ratio

**Leverage:**
- Debt/Equity
- Interest coverage
- Debt/EBITDA

**Efficiency:**
- Asset turnover
- Inventory turnover
- Days sales outstanding

### Phase 6: Valuation Context

**Objective:** Assess current market valuation.

1. Fetch current quote and overview
2. Calculate/retrieve:
   - P/E (trailing and forward)
   - P/S, P/B, EV/EBITDA
   - PEG ratio

3. Compare to:
   - Historical averages
   - Industry peers
   - Market averages

### Phase 7: Synthesis

**Objective:** Form a coherent investment thesis.

Summarize findings:
1. **Strengths:** What's working well financially?
2. **Weaknesses:** What are the concerns?
3. **Valuation:** Fair, expensive, or cheap?
4. **Key Metrics to Monitor:** What should be watched going forward?
