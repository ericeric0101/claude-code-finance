## Business, audit and annual risks

Apple sells integrated hardware and related services. Competitive pricing, short product cycles, third-party developer support and outsourced manufacturing constrain its ecosystem advantages. Single-source components and concentrated Asian assembly create supply and geopolitical exposure. These disclosures support assessing Apple as a mixed consumer-device/platform company rather than a pure software peer. This classification is an analytical inference. [FY2025 10-K, Items 1/1A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm).

Ernst & Young issued unqualified opinions on consolidated financial statements and ICFR, dated 2025-10-31; auditor tenure began in 2009. The critical audit matter is uncertain tax positions: gross unrecognized benefits USD23.2bn, of which USD10.6bn would affect the effective tax rate if recognized. A CAM identifies estimation complexity and is not a qualified opinion. Cybersecurity escalation and audit-committee oversight are disclosed. These statements do not establish absence of fraud or future control failures. [FY2025 10-K, Item 1C; audit reports, pp.49–51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm).

## Latest quarter: material diligence findings

Refunds are recorded as reduced products cost of sales. Management identifies memory-cost pressure, intensifying semiconductor/NAND/DRAM shortages and AI compute constraints. R&D growth reflects AI infrastructure and headcount; product growth includes iPhone Pro mix and Mac laptops. Services growth reflects advertising/cloud. These are management explanations, not independently measured unit-volume decomposition. [Q3 10-Q, MD&A pp.13–18; risk factors pp.21–23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm).

App Store commissions face DMA/Epic litigation; Google search-distribution licensing is exposed to antitrust appeals. Manufacturing commitments total USD57.0bn, USD56.2bn payable within twelve months; other purchases total USD29.3bn, USD9.2bn within twelve months. Treat these as operating commitments, not automatically interest-bearing debt. Management reported effective disclosure controls at 2026-06-27 and no material ICFR changes during Q3. [Q3 10-Q, MD&A pp.18–19; Part II Items 1/1A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm).

The release reports revenue USD109.4bn, gross margin 50.1% and diluted EPS USD2.02. Tariff refunds contributed approximately two percentage points to margin and USD0.11 to EPS. Simple analyst normalization: margin approximately **48.1%**, EPS approximately **USD1.91**. These subtraction estimates are not a complete issuer non-GAAP reconciliation. No exact refund dollar amount was established here; avoid inferring an exact amount from rounded margin guidance. [Apple Q3 release, 2026-07-30](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-reports-third-quarter-results/).

### Additional Standard findings

**Accounting comparability:** FY2025 net-profit growth is helped by the prior-year Ireland state-aid tax charge: FY2024 contained a USD10.246bn net charge; FY2025 contained a USD486m benefit related to that decision. Treat the rebound separately from operating growth. Prior-year escrow was restricted and should not be silently counted as freely available cash in a historical EV bridge. FY2023 was 53 weeks; FY2024/FY2025 were 52. [FY2025 10-K, Notes 1/4/7](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm).

**Working capital and commitments:** Q3 inventory was USD11.092bn versus USD5.718bn at FY2025 year-end. Components rose to USD7.645bn from USD2.124bn while finished goods declined to USD3.447bn from USD3.594bn. This is component accumulation, not evidence by itself of unsold finished-device demand collapse. Trade receivables' largest customer concentration rose to 18% from 12%; two vendors account for 47% and 22% of vendor non-trade receivables. Procurement, collectability and obsolescence merit monitoring. Net intangible assets were USD25.417bn versus USD13.301bn; this review did not establish the transaction-level explanation. Avoid attributing the entire change to an acquisition without evidence. [Q3 10-Q, Notes 4/5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm).

**Economic dilution:** Nine-month FY2026 SBC expense was USD10.523bn; unrecognized RSU compensation was USD26.7bn over a weighted-average 2.7 years. Cash-flow SBC addbacks do not eliminate the economic cost. The proxy reports 151,604,285 securities issuable under outstanding awards and 1,051,241,204 plan securities remaining available at 2025-09-27; remaining authorization is not immediate dilution, and full-value awards can consume two authorization units per share. [Q3 10-Q Note 8](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm), [2026 proxy, p.83](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000130817926000008/aapl014016-def14a.htm).

**Segment limitation:** Geographic operating income excludes corporate R&D and G&A; it is not a product-level economic profit table. ASU2023-07 disclosures were retrospectively adopted in FY2025. Assets are not supplied to the CODM for segment assessment, so segment capital employed and segment ROCE are unavailable. Do not derive Services operating profit from its gross margin by assuming zero overhead. [FY2025 10-K Notes 1/13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm).

**History versus latest momentum:** FY2024 MD&A attributes China weakness to iPhone/iPad and FX and Services growth to advertising/App Store/cloud. FY2025 China remained weak while Services continued expanding; FY2026 Q3 China improved with iPhone and favorable RMB. This establishes a recovery from earlier weakness, not proof of permanent share gains. Apple does not provide sufficient units/ASP data here for a full price-versus-volume bridge. No falsifiable numeric forward guidance was found in the annual MD&A, so a numerical promise-delivery claim is unavailable. [FY2024 MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm), [FY2025 MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm), [Q3 FY2026 MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm).

**Pay incentives and minority rights:** Cook's target FY2025 package was USD59m; his USD50m target equity mix was 75% performance/25% time-based. Performance RSUs use relative TSR versus S&P500; cash bonuses use equally weighted GAAP sales and operating income, rather than ROIC/FCF. Maximum cash awards were earned. Hedging/pledging are prohibited for directors/executives and recoupment policies exist. FY2025 audit fees USD24.703m out of total EY fees USD34.277m; all services were preapproved. These are disclosed safeguards, not an independent certification of governance quality. Ownership footnotes are older than the January table: Vanguard shares refer to June 2025; BlackRock shares refer to December 2023. The percentages must not be labelled October 2026 holdings. [2026 proxy, pp.38–53,65–66,81–83](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000130817926000008/aapl014016-def14a.htm).

**Observed dissent:** At the February 2026 meeting, executive compensation passed with approximately 8.60% votes against among For+Against votes; Levinson had approximately 9.02% opposition on that same denominator. Abstentions/broker non-votes are excluded from these analyst-calculated percentages. Auditor appointment and director-plan amendments passed. This is actual voting evidence, not the proxy's previous-year support figure. [2026 meeting 8-K, Item 5.07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000114036126006577/ef20060722_8k.htm).

**Incorporated risks and group map:** Q2 explicitly adds AI legal/product harm, cyberattack amplification, and return-on-investment risks; Q3 adds intensifying component/compute supply constraints. The subsidiaries exhibit identifies significant Ireland/Singapore and other operating entities; minor subsidiaries are omitted under SEC rules, so this is not a complete legal-entity inventory. [Q2 risk factors](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000013/aapl-20260328.htm), [subsidiaries exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/a10-kexhibit21109272025.htm).
