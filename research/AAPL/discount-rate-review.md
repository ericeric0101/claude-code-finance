# AAPL discount-rate review — 2026-10-11

**Interpretation:** the available nominal USD anchors support an indicative **9.56% WACC** (rounded central modelling rate **9.5%–10.0%**). Use **8.5%–11.5%** as a valuation sensitivity envelope, explicitly an assumption rather than a confidence interval or observed market WACC. A 9.0% rate remains a favourable sensitivity case; it should not be presented as the empirically derived central rate.

**Calculation:** nominal USD cost of equity = 5.24% + 1.0460 × 4.20% = **9.633%**. Assumed marginal pre-tax debt cost = 5.24% + 0.475% = **5.715%**. WACC = 9.633% × 98.5334% + 5.715% × (1−21%) × 1.4666% = **9.558%**. Raw beta instead gives **9.652%**. These results are calculated conditional estimates, not observable facts; small debt weight means beta and ERP matter much more than precise debt cost.

**Beta reproducibility:** prices were fetched using the existing finance-skill Python environment and `yfinance.Ticker(...).history(start='2021-09-01', end='2026-10-10', auto_adjust=True)`. Remove time zones, align AAPL/SPY dates, retain month-end observations through September 2026, and calculate simple monthly percentage changes. Beta is sample covariance divided by SPY sample variance. The incomplete October month is excluded. R² is **0.4588**; conventional OLS slope standard error is **0.1524**, with an approximate normal 95% interval **0.770–1.367** for raw beta. This interval describes historical-regression estimation noise under OLS assumptions; it is not a forecast-risk or WACC confidence interval. Monthly return rows are preserved in [discount-rate-inputs.json](discount-rate-inputs.json). A business-mix bottom-up beta has not been established; combining Dell, HP and Microsoft mechanically would falsely homogenize Apple's hardware/services mix.

**Convention:** the central calculation uses Treasury plus the associated unadjusted US implied premium convention. Damodaran also identifies a 0.22% sovereign default adjustment: reducing Treasury by 0.22% requires increasing the ERP by 0.22%, rather than subtracting sovereign risk without reconciliation. With the adjusted beta, this changes cost of equity by only about 0.010 percentage points. Do not combine the lower adjusted Treasury with an unchanged premium. [NYU convention](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/home.htm)

**Debt guardrail:** Apple's FY2025 notes table discloses 2025 issuance effective rates **4.07%–4.83%** and legacy effective rates **0.03%–5.75%**; those accounting rates are historical issuance/hedging evidence, not October 2026 marginal borrowing yields. The latest 10-Q provides fair value and carrying amount but no contemporaneous portfolio YTM. Never replace marginal debt cost with a legacy effective rate or back-solve one from note fair value without maturities and coupon cash flows. [FY2025 10-K, Note 9](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm)

**Sensitivity recommendation:** run WACC 8.5%, 9.5%, 10.0%, 10.5%, 11.5% against terminal growth 2.0%, 2.5%, 3.0%, 3.5%. Keep cash-flow forecasts identical when isolating this sensitivity; operational/regulatory losses already modelled in bear cash flows should not automatically receive another arbitrary discount-rate surcharge. The envelope reflects modelling uncertainty in beta, premium and duration; its edges are assumptions. Current Apple bond YTM/rating confirmation and October share/debt values remain disclosed gaps.
