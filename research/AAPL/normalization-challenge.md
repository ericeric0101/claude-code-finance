Scope is the user-requested cashflow/valuation completion, not full peer-rating completion. The challenger read AGENTS.md, the challenge reference, draft report, archived primary statements, source inputs and `scripts/normalize_aapl.py`; independently recomputed financial bridges and DCF without importing its valuation function. ## Independently verified bridge

TTM is FY2025 + 9M FY2026 − 9M FY2025, ending 2026-06-27. The primary statements reproduce CFO **146,724** as NI **128,930** + D&A **13,100** + SBC **13,706** − other noncash **378** − operating assets/liabilities cash use **8,634**. Within that cash use: trade AR −4,051; vendor AR −8,231; inventory −5,284; other assets −19,347; AP +14,178; other liabilities +14,101. PP&E capex **10,041**, reported CFO-capex **136,683**, and post-SBC diagnostic **122,977** reconcile. [FY2025 SEC cashflow statement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm), [FY2026 Q3 SEC statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm).

Starting from that diagnostic, the revised assumed operating proxy reconciles exactly:

`122,977 + 8,634 + 378 + (125,189.9412 − 128,930) + (8,000 − 13,100) − (12,715 − 10,041) = 120,474.9412`.

The adjustments remove historical balance movements/noncash residual, replace reported NI with approximate normalized operating NOPAT, replace total D&A with modeled PP&E depreciation, and substitute full FY2025 capex. This is a **transparent normalization scenario**, not a separately reported or fully audited FCFF figure. The 18% operating tax, zero normalized WC cash credit, and 8,000 forward depreciation are assumptions. Gross other-asset movements cannot all be certified as disposable tax timing. Historical trade WC and rapidly growing intangibles justify downside reinvestment stress.

The tariff source specifically supports the rounded margin/EPS adjustments, not the exact estimated dollar amount. [Apple July 30 release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-reports-third-quarter-results/). Primary tax evidence supports separating the Ireland charge/benefit from the later escrow settlement; no additional legacy cash-tax amount is added to the already normalized NOPAT route.

## Valuation, growth funding and remaining constraints

Independent sums reproduce base EV **2,797,565.4292**, equity value **2,849,738.4292**, value **193.6664/share**, terminal contribution **43.7951%**, and the revised matched reverse growth **14.80518%**. The equity bridge adds surplus liquidity **136,517** after the **assumed 10,000 operating reserve**, subtracts financing debt **84,344**, and divides by diluted share proxy **14,714.676m**. The operating reserve is not a disclosed requirement, and the June balance/share dates remain stale relative to October price.

Primary driver value comes from normalized operating earnings **and assumed future reinvestment**, not mechanically compounding the 120,474.9412 normalization output. Central first-year NOPAT is **135,205.1365**, reinvestment **10,816.4109**, FCFF **124,388.7256**; its floor is only 5,092.2. Terminal NOPAT **355,135.2005**, reinvestment **53,270.2801**, and FCFF **301,864.9204** imply a 15% terminal reinvestment ratio. The 100%/20% ROIIC assumptions should be defended with operating evidence, not the old financing-route ROIC.

The `NOPAT × g / ROIIC` formula is a stylized forward reinvestment-budget relation. Tests that FCFF plus investment equals NOPAT check arithmetic only; they do not prove actual incremental capital earns the stipulated return, that capital earns within the same year, or that the fade's annually changing growth/return timing has been empirically calibrated. This distinction belongs in the forecast limitations. The cash-budget floor is deliberately a minimum, not proof that hidden licensing/AI investment is absent.

Operating leases remain expensed in EBIT and are not arbitrarily added to debt; that is coherent. Finance-lease debt/principal is a separate small unresolved perimeter item: the policy text says it is separately stressed, but a quantified finance-lease-specific stress was not present in the reviewed output. Either provide it or replace “stressed” with “not quantified.” Do not claim that a PP&E/WC stress is a lease reconciliation.

The earnings-multiple method now discounts its FY2027 endpoint to the research date using cost of equity and includes a stated dividend assumption. Independently reproduced normalized EPS **8.56617**, FY2027 horizon **0.95551 years**, and present-value bear/base/bull **173.5469/241.5651/318.4569**. Net non-operating income is correctly included for this equity method, separate from operating DCF. The retained exit P/Es are judgment inputs, not another independent proof of fair value.

## Verdict consequence and falsification

**Verdict change:** the old unsupported $110bn anchor is replaced by a reproducible approximately **$120.5bn modeled normalized operating cashflow** with clear accounting treatment. A conditional valuation analysis is now supportable; it does not establish a definitive intrinsic-value point estimate. Initial D&A/reinvestment/share-target objections required real corrections. Remaining sustainability, growth and ROIIC uncertainty should widen the range rather than block all scenario analysis or be resolved in the thesis's favor.

**Single-input sensitivity:** holding central economics fixed, WACC approximately **6.839%** matches price; holding rates/growth fixed would require an implausibly high **57.30% EBIT margin**. But the joint bullish case above flips at 8.5% WACC, so the bear/base central result is not universal. Neither the central nor favorable discount rate is an observed guarantee of required return.

**What would change my mind:** a sourced current cash-investment/amortization rollforward, demonstrated growth capital efficiency and persistent refund-excluded margins, or evidence that the new device/AI cycle can sustain the revenue growth implied by the reverse model. Sustained low incremental returns, recurring operating-asset cash uses beyond budget, or structurally weaker Services economics would lower value.

**Most likely way wrong:** the model understates cash investment needed for AI/licensing/intangibles or overstates the duration of exceptionally high incremental returns. Alternatively it could miss a genuine premium-product growth cycle and apply a central growth assumption too low. Normalized accounting alone does not adjudicate that forecast disagreement.

**Searched but not established:** exact current PP&E-only TTM depreciation, exact separate tariff-refund cash, current gross financing interest/securities income split and current intangible cash-addition rollforward were sought in filings/companyfacts but not established. The operating-EBIT route avoids needing a fabricated interest split; the other gaps remain disclosed assumptions. No exhaustive channel/short-report/legal investigation was performed in this focused task.
