# PL peer, market and macro review — 2026-10-11

Scope: targeted review for the PL research dated 2026-10-11. Latest filed quarter is Q2 FY2027 (quarter ended 2026-07-31); market price uses the latest completed NYSE session before the research date. Facts below are not forecasts unless marked as such.

## Price and operating context

**Fact.** PL closed at **$17.18 on 2026-10-09** (NYSE; USD), per [historical price data](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pl/history/). The company’s [Q2 FY2027 results](https://investors.planet.com/news/news-details/2026/Planet-Reports-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2027/default.aspx), issued 2026-09-03, report $116.1m revenue (+58% y/y), 57% GAAP gross margin, $13.9m adjusted EBITDA, and a $9.4m GAAP net loss. Six-month operating cash flow was $68.4m, but capex and capitalized software of $47.1m left $21.3m reported FCF, versus $54.3m in the prior-year half. Adjusted FCF adds back $7.5m of litigation settlement payments. Backlog was $814.9m and RPO $753.1m at July 31; management expects 46% of RPO and 50% of backlog recognized in the coming 12 months. Backlog includes cancellable contract value and unfunded written orders, so it is not equivalent to contracted revenue. Backlog declined from $900.4m on Jan 31.

**Dilution / funding. Fact.** PL sold 3.78m shares under its ATM program in Q2, raising $122.4m net at an average $31.95/share, and also received $107.8m from warrant exercises in the half; cash plus short-term investments reached $865.4m. The Oct 9 close is ~46% below the disclosed ATM average. This bolsters liquidity but demonstrates meaningful dilution and the risk of financing growth by issuing equity. See the company’s [release](https://investors.planet.com/news/news-details/2026/Planet-Reports-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2027/default.aspx) and [SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1836833/000119312526382016/pl-20260731.htm).

## Public peer check

**Fact / interpretation.** BlackSky (NYSE: BKSY) is the closest currently listed imagery/intelligence peer located in this targeted screen, but it is not a clean comparable: its emphasis is high-resolution, tactical ISR and AI analytics, while Planet sells frequent broad-area imagery, recurring data access, and dedicated satellite services. BlackSky reported Q2 2026 revenue of $33.3m (+50% y/y), $25m space-based intelligence and AI revenue, $4.7m adjusted EBITDA (14.2% margin), and $244.1m cash/investments after issuing $150m of ATM equity. It reaffirmed FY2026 revenue guidance of $130–150m and capex of $50–60m. These issuer figures are useful for industry demand and funding context, not a direct valuation multiple comparison. Source: [BlackSky Q2 results](https://blacksky.com/press-releases/blacksky-reports-second-quarter-2026-results/).

Maxar, another important commercial imagery operator, became privately held after Advent completed its acquisition in May 2023; its former public shares ceased trading. It therefore supplies business context but no current public valuation multiple. Source: [Advent acquisition completion](https://www.adventinternational.com/news/u-s-private-equity-firm-advent-international-and-bci-complete-acquisition-of-maxar-technologies/).
